KENWOOD speakers are designed to meet the critical listening demands of the North American music lover. On the subwoofer, the newly designed cone features ribbing that adds strength to the cone and minimizes distortion. The Kenwood Amps power your entire system with a single 5-channel amplifier. This powerful one amplifier solution for front and rear speakers plus subwoofers, is an easy solution for any vehicle to enhance your factory sound system.
